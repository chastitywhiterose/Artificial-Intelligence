# Autistic Articles Against AI

Collection of writings about the Dangers of Artificial Intelligence

I had decided not to write a book in favor of Artificial Intelligence or against it. But when I saw it shoved into my face on all social media, and that even Linux users and developers were being led astray, I felt it was right to write this short book.

I want to stand with those who speak against AI slop and warn believers in Free Software to stay watchful. I could hardly believe that even Linus Torvalds could be deceived enough to share in their suffering and confusion.

Yet the devil is the god of this world, and where the Natural Intelligence given by God is missing, Satan has found a new vessel of Artificial Intelligence to overpower and deceive both the weak and the strong. May God protect us. Amen.

Furthermore, I have decided that at no part shall I use Artificial Intelligence in the making of this book. Instead, I want to educate people on the real problems posed by AI that are not being talked about enough.

My personal issue with AI is not that it is a machine, but that history is being altered. Books long out of print are being scanned into Large Language Models before the books are destroyed. I pray that it is not too late for others to look back in history and see that the people burning books are never the good guys.
